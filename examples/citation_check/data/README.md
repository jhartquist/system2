# The data of `citation_check`

`citations.json` and `rfc7519.txt` come from the
[`cookbooks/citation_check`](https://github.com/typesafe-ai/typesafe-public-examples/tree/2adcf82/cookbooks/citation_check)
folder of TypeSafe's typesafe-public-examples, at commit `2adcf82`. The files are not changed.

- `citations.json` is the work of TypeSafe, under the MIT license in `LICENSE`. Its quotes are
  from RFC 7519, except the quote of `sig_reporting`.
- `rfc7519.txt` is [RFC 7519](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7519.txt), *JSON Web Token
  (JWT)*, Copyright (c) 2015 IETF Trust and the persons identified as the document authors. It
  is under the [IETF Trust Legal Provisions](https://trustee.ietf.org/license-info).
